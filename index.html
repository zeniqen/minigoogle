PRE-1998 SEARCH ENGINE

The "Old Way": Keyword Frequency

Formula: Score = Term Frequency (TF) in page

Vulnerability: Keyword Stuffing

A spammer repeating the keyword 10 times easily hijacks position #1, regardless of quality or legitimacy.

Showing results Sorted by: Keyword Count (High to Low)
GOOGLE INNOVATION (1998)

The "New Way": PageRank Algorithm

Formula: PR(A) = (1-d)/N + d ∑(PR(T)/C(T)) (d = 0.85)

Defense: Democratic Link Authority

PageRank ignores repetition and ranks pages by authoritative incoming votes from other reputable pages.

Showing results Sorted by: PageRank Score (High to Low)

Why did PageRank change the internet?

1
Hyperlinks as democratic votes: A link from an authoritative food guide is a verified peer review.
2
Spam neutralized: Page 6 stuffed "pizza" 10 times, but with zero inbound links, its PageRank is trapped at the bare mathematical minimum (0.025).
3
Quality elevated: Page 2 (the actual pizza restaurant) ranks #1 because 4 independent sites link to it, yielding 39.3% of the total authority.
